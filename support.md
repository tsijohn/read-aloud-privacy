# Read Books Aloud Support

For help with Read Books Aloud, or questions about your account or your
family's information, email
**[utjohnkkim@gmail.com](mailto:utjohnkkim@gmail.com)**.

To help us answer, please include:

- your iPhone model and iOS version,
- the app version (Read Books Aloud 1.0), and
- what you were doing when the problem happened.

Please never send your password.

## Your information

How the app handles your information, including the outside AI services it
uses, is described in the
[Privacy Policy](https://tsijohn.github.io/read-aloud-privacy/). To ask about
your information or to request its deletion, email the address above.
